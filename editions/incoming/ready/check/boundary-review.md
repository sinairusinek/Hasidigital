## Question of principle

Many numbered items are not tales. 74 items in Eser-Orot and 46 in Eser-Kedushot are under 250 characters: a saying, a trait (for example "(יז) והי' לו ג״כ כח הצדקה…"), or a citation from another book. Do we keep the rule *one numbered item = one story division* and let the tagging mark such items as sayings, or should items that are not narratives become non-story? I would keep the rule, because it is the author's own division and is reproducible, and mark genre at the tagging stage.
