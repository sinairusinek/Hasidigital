HTR Test

The overnight test: can a vision model read our books as well as the Transkribus model (52805), and can its reading help fix the tags and the lines? Everything is scored against the RA's corrected pages (FINAL) in set A, 209 pages from 13 books. No Transkribus credits were spent. Claude ran on the Max plan through claude -p; Gemini 3.8 Flash ran on the Google key.

In short. Claude Opus reads almost as well as Transkribus: 1.6% of characters wrong, against 0.84%. Part of that gap is not real, because FINAL began as Transkribus output. Gemini Flash is much worse at 6.8%. The reading adds nothing to the tags that the picture alone does not give, with one exception: running heads, page numbers and catch-words. For those, comparing the text across consecutive pages halves the errors. It barely helps the lines.

The FINAL bias. The RA started from the Transkribus text and corrected it. Wherever a Transkribus mistake was left in, FINAL agrees with Transkribus and counts against Claude. You can see this by book: on שבע רצון Transkribus scores 0.0% and on one volume of תורת הרמ"ל 0.1%. That only happens if the RA hardly changed anything. On the books where Transkribus has visible errors, Claude is level or better: עשר אורות 1.7% against 2.0%, בית ישראל 1.3% against 1.6%, ספורי אנשי שם 1.1% against 1.0%. Claude's weakest book is תורת הרמ"ל השלם, at 3.1% against 0.7%.

Gemini. Gemini 3.8 Flash makes four times as many errors as Claude, and 80 of its 204 pages are over 5%. Gemini 2.5 Pro loses the line numbering and was dropped after 26 pages. There is no Gemini 3 Pro for text, only an image model.

3. Can the reading correct the tags and the lines?

Three ways the reading could help the layout. (a) In one call the model reads the lines on our layout and also returns the tags and a list of bad lines (cut, missing, merged). (b) Rules use the text across consecutive pages: a top line that recurs on other pages is a running head; a short top line that reads as a Hebrew numeral is a page number; a single word at the bottom that matches the next page's first word is a catch-word. (c) Lines the model calls "part of a line" are joined to their neighbour on the same baseline.

The flags find most bad lines but cannot fix them. A missing or merged line needs a new cut, not a new tag. They are useful as a pointer to the pages you should look at.

4. What I would do next

  • Reading. Claude Opus is a real alternative to Transkribus HTR for these books. It costs nothing on Max, against about half a credit per page, but it is slower, about a minute per page. Before deciding, please settle the 20 lines above. If your verdicts match mine, I would run Claude and Transkribus side by side on one new book, compare where they differ, and look only at those lines.
  • Tags. Production order: YOLO, Claude relabelling with a book note, then the text rules for running heads, page numbers and catch-words.
  • Lines. The reading does not repair lines. The remaining 6% of wrong lines need either a Kraken model trained on our RA lines or the Transkribus line model. Training Kraken is the next experiment.
  • Still respected: no HTR on the new books, no Transkribus credits spent, nothing deleted.
