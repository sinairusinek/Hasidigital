**READ FIRST — these rules take precedence over the instructions further down.**
Further down you will read "preserve existing annotations", "confirm the entity as-is", "always preserve the full entity span as annotated" and "do NOT add entities speculatively". Those sentences mean: do not DELETE a real entity, and do not invent one. They do NOT mean: keep a wrong boundary, or leave an obvious date, book title or name untagged. The existing spans come from DictaBERT, which very often cuts names and dates short; your main job is to repair those spans and to add the obvious entities it missed:

1. **Repair spans that are cut short or cut wrong.** DictaBERT often cuts off the first or last letters of a name. Compare every span with the passage and extend or trim it to the whole name. Examples of cut spans that must be repaired: "ראד" where the text has "בראד", "אהליב" for "מאהליב", "אנצוט" for "לאנצוט" (in "מלאנצוט"), "ינאווא" for "שינאווא", "תקנ״" for "תקנ״ט", "ו שבט" for "ט״ו שבט".
2. **Strip a prefix letter ONLY when it is a separate particle.** After אבד״ק / אב״ד דק״ק / האבד״ק the town name follows directly ("אבד״ק X" = rabbi of the community of X), so a ב or מ right after these titles is normally the first letter of the town name. Many East European place names themselves begin with ב, מ, ל, ש, ה, ו or כ (בראד, בוטשאטש, בארדיטשוב, מאהליב, לעלוב, לאנצוט, לובלין, שאץ, שינאווא, הורודנא, וויזניץ...). In "מלאנצוט" strip only the first מ ("from"); in "בבארדיטשוב" strip only the first ב. If stripping a letter leaves a string that is not a real place name, the letter belongs to the name — keep it.
3. **Dates: tag the whole date.** Every existing date span that is only a month name or only part of a year MUST be widened to the full date written in the text ("כ״ב אלול", not "אלול"). Examples of full dates: "ט״ו שבט", "ער״ח מנחם אב", "ט״ו שבט תקנ״ט", "שנת תקנ״ט". Add any Hebrew calendar date or gematria year that appears in the passage and is not yet tagged. A bare month or weekday used generically ("בשבת", "בחורף") is not a date entity.
4. **Book titles: add them.** Titles of books and works named in the passage are obvious entities even when DictaBERT missed them: e.g. "מאור עינים", "תולדות יעקב יוסף", "דגל מחנה אפרים", "לקוטי מוהר״ן", tractates like "מס׳ שבת". Add every such title that is clearly a book name. Do not tag chapter/section references (e.g. "פ״ג מ״ב", "דף י״ב") or bare citation fragments.
5. **Named people that were missed: add them** when the passage clearly names a person (a personal name, a well-known title-name like "הבעש״ט", or a rabbinic abbreviation like "מהרש״א", "הרמ״ע").
6. **Junk: remove** spans that are only punctuation, a single letter, or a stray fragment of an abbreviation (e.g. a lone "״" plus one letter, a lone letter, or a cut-off title like "מהר״" with no name), unless the fragment is itself a whole name abbreviation.

Being complete matters as much as being careful: a real date, book title or name that you leave untagged is an error too.
