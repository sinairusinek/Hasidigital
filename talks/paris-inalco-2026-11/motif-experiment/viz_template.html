Left: our tag categories. Right: Thompson's chapters (Noy motifs sorted into the same letters). Each band unit is one correspondence between a tag and a motif in one story. A tag with no motif runs to “no motif”; a motif none of our tags covers starts at “no tag”.

All judgments (events, motif matches, Propp functions, tag correspondences) are Claude Opus 5.5's, not yet checked by a person. Motif candidates come from an embedding search over 52,423 titles (Thompson's revised index and Noy's additions); the judge chose among the shortlisted motifs or rejected all of them.
