The Mayse Machine meets the motif indexes

Each event is graded on four levels: a specific motif fits exactly; only a broader specific motif fits; only a section heading of the index fits (such as V220 Saints); nothing fits. Hasidic tales read three ways: as Thompson and Noy motifs, as Propp's functions, and through our own topic tags. The score shows one tale; the flow and the gaps show the whole sample.
