# Numbers for the article — post-audit recomputation (2026-07-21)

Source: `editions/online/*.xml`, the 9 core editions, **post-audit corpus**
(RA tags + LLM-audited additions after the precision-audit removals;
commits `d5c1891` → `815d694` → `f81b07b`), with the 5-tier women scheme.

Two definitions of "women present", per the team decision:
- **any** — any reference incl. `mention_only`
- **char** — women as characters (`major` + `catalyst` + `minor`), excl. `mention_only`

## Corrections to specific sentences in the draft

1. **Pidyon** — draft: *"out of the six hundred and fifty stories, only seven
   address the practice of pidyon … pidyon is never depicted without female
   characters."*
   Now: **23 stories** carry `pidyon_nefesh`; **22 of 23 (96%)** include women
   (single exception: `Khal-Hasidim_0205`); **15 of 23 (65%)** have women as
   characters. "Never depicted without women" must be softened to
   "all but one". A separate practice, `pidyon_shvuyim` (redemption of
   captives), has 12 stories (8 any / 4 char) and should not be conflated.
   Pidyon-nefesh stories by edition: Kehal Hasidim 11, Shivhei ha-Besht 3,
   Adat Tsadikim 3, Pe'er mi-Kdoshim 2, Mif'alot 2, Sipurei Tsadikim 1,
   Ma'ase Tsadikim 1.

2. **Poverty** — draft: *"Out of the twenty-two stories addressing poverty as
   virtue, sixteen present female characters."* The tag is now `social:poverty`:
   **25 stories, 20 with women (80%) — and all 20 as characters** (no
   mention-only cases). So: "twenty of twenty-five".

3. **Business advice**: 9 stories, **100% (9/9)** include women (any);
   5/9 (56%) as characters.

4. **Solitude**: 19 stories, 89% any (17/19) / **74% char (14/19)** — the
   wives-as-breadwinners reading survives the recomputation.

5. **Agunot**: `releasing_agunot` 20 stories, 90% any / 80% char;
   the `agunah` character tag is 100% (7/7) as expected.

6. **The inverse (masculine-elite) cluster** — still sharp:

## Caveats (must accompany any published statistic)

- The LLM-audited tag layer: candidate retrieval recall was estimated at
  **87–93% per category** (funnel evaluation), so tag counts are floors, not
  exact censuses; the Opus adjudication pass was benchmarked against a
  100-story human anchor but **not fully human-validated**.
- Several category definitions were patched mid-audit (weak-definitions gap);
  tags audited under the patched definitions.
- The 5-tier women annotation (incl. the mention-only/character distinction)
  is Claude-annotated with per-annotation confidence; RA-annotated only in the
  original binary scheme. The mention-only boundary is the annotation's
  least-stable tier.
- ~50 anomalous tag tokens (taxonomy-consolidation backlog) are excluded from
  all figures; tag renames pending from the definitions review
  (Chen/Gadi sheet) could shift small-n counts.
